## English

**Subtitle (Apple, max 30 chars):**
Place blocks, pop the lines!

**Short description (Google, max 80 chars):**
Drop colorful blocks, pop rows & columns, chain combos! No timer, no stress.

**Description:**
Meet Blokko, a candy-colored block puzzle that's fun for every age!

🧩 **Easy to learn, hard to put down:** Drag pieces onto the 8x8 board. Fill a row or column and watch it burst into color!

🔥 **Chain combos:** Pop lines back to back to multiply your score. Clear a row and a column at once for a big celebration.

📅 **Daily Puzzle:** A fresh board and goal every day, the same for everyone. Hit the target and earn your star!

🎨 **5 themes:** Candy, Ocean, Night, Garden and Dream.

🐣 **A cute mascot:** Blokko cheers, gasps and even sulks along with every move!

✨ **No timer, no stress:** Take your time, plan ahead, quit anytime and continue where you left off.

📴 **Play offline:** On a plane, on the subway, anywhere.

🔒 **No ads, no data collection:** No account needed. No personal data is collected.
